About Us

The Digital Safe Space platform is an initiative of the State Department for Gender and Affirmative Action, a government department responsible for coordinating national efforts to promote gender equality, protect vulnerable populations, and eliminate gender-based violence in Kenya. The department operates under the Ministry of Gender, Culture, the Arts and Heritage and plays a central role in developing policies, programmes, and partnerships that advance gender equality and social inclusion across the country. Through its work with national and county governments, civil society organizations, and development partners, the department supports initiatives aimed at strengthening survivor protection systems, promoting women’s empowerment, and addressing gender-based violence both offline and in digital spaces.

Understanding Technology-Facilitated Gender-Based Violence

TFGBV involves the use of digital tools—such as social media, messaging apps, and tracking devices—to harass, stalk, or abuse individuals. Recognizing these patterns is the first step toward safety and digital bodily autonomy.

Doxing

The act of publishing someone's private or personal information online without their consent.

Cyber Stalking

The use of online platforms to harass, threaten or intimidate someone repeatedly, with the intent to cause emotional distress.

Cyber Mob

A large group of people online who collectively attack individuals or groups through online forums and social media.

Our Role in Addressing Gender-Based Violence

The State Department leads national efforts to prevent and respond to gender-based violence, driving inclusive policies and programmes that protect vulnerable communities across Kenya.

The department also works with multiple stakeholders to ensure that gender equality principles are integrated into national development planning and public service delivery.

Our key areas of action

  • 📋
    Developing and implementing gender policies and programmes
  • 🤝
    Coordinating national strategies to prevent sexual and gender-based violence
  • 🛡️
    Supporting survivor protection and referral systems
  • 📣
    Promoting public awareness and community engagement on gender equality
  • 🏛️
    Strengthening institutional responses to violence against women and girls
